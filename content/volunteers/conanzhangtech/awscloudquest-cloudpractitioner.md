> ### **About this Award and Achievement**
> <hr>
> 
> AWS Cloud Quest: Cloud Practitioner is a role-based learning experience designed to help you start your cloud career or transition from a non-cloud role. Practice customer conversations through AI-powered dialogue and live feedback, then make sound technical decisions as you build and validate AWS solutions in a live environment. Featuring 12 FREE hands-on assignments, this course guides you through building real-world solutions with instructional videos, architecture diagrams, and knowledge quizzes. Explore foundational cloud concepts such as compute, networking, security, and storage while developing practical skills for your cloud career.
