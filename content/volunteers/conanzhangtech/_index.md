## **About Me**

Conan is a hands-on learner who is deeply passionate about bridging the gap between technology and community service. As the Co-Founder and President of Isolated Command, he is grateful to work alongside people who share a common goal: supporting local non-profits and leveraging our young talents to strengthen our nation's digital roadmap

Recognising that charities often do the hardest work in our society without the resources for modern, secure IT systems, Conan and Jia Jian start Isolated Command. They believe that technology should be accessible, reliable, and secure—making everyday tasks easier for the volunteers and social workers on the ground.

With a strong interest in IT and cybersecurity, Conan has spent years developing his skills through industry certifications, hackathons, and community projects. However, he is always the first to admit that he is a lifelong learner. Whether he is configuring a network, mentoring a peer, or volunteering at a local event, he focuses on practical problem-solving rather than technical jargon.

At his core, Conan believes that technical skills are most valuable when they are used to support and uplift others.
